    Read this chart correctly

    • BNSF and BHE are net PP&E only, not full business asset totals or valuations.
    • OXY is the equity-method carrying value of common stock. Its preferred stock and warrants remain in Other equities.
    • Cash includes cash equivalents. The T-Bills slice excludes bills already inside cash equivalents. Restricted cash remains in Other.
    • GOOGL means Alphabet Class A only. Other equities includes non-13F assets, other stocks and any undisclosed confidential positions.
    • The balance sheet mixes GAAP carrying values and fair values. This is not an economic valuation or investment recommendation.
    • 13F reports are normally filed within 45 days of quarter end. Options and principal-amount instruments are not ordinary stock slices.
